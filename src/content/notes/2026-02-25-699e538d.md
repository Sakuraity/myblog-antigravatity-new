What is lethal about this lack of belief (in dynamism, human choice, and markets), is that it inevitably leads to misplaced prognostication about the future. If the fundamental assumption underlying the analysis is that things are as they are, then one can only envision breaking them; however, if you can see how things developed and changed, then you can have the humility to appreciate how things might develop and change in the future, even in the face of something as potentially disruptive as AI.

这种对动态、人类选择和市场的信念缺失的致命之处在于，它必然导致对未来的错误预测。如果分析的基本假设是事物现状不变，那么人们就只能设想如何打破现状；然而，如果你能够看到事物是如何发展和变化的，那么你就能谦逊地理解事物未来可能的发展和变化，即便面对像人工智能这样具有潜在颠覆性的事物。
